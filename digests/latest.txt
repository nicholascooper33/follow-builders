The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
"Cloudflare CEO: Bot Takeover, Edge AI & The Hard Decision Every CEO Will Face"

Takeaway: Bot and AI agent traffic now outnumbers human traffic on the internet — and the implications for infrastructure, business models, and workforce planning are just beginning to unfold.

Matthew Prince, cofounder and CEO of Cloudflare, joined Matt Turck for a wide-ranging conversation that opens with a landmark data point: in the first half of 2026, automated traffic (bots and AI agents) officially surpassed human traffic on the internet for the first time. As recently as fall 2025, Cloudflare had projected this crossover wouldn't happen until late 2027 — it arrived more than a year early.

Prince explains the driver clearly: "If I go to ChatGPT or Claude and I'm shopping for a digital camera, you're seeing thousands of times more site visits for that same activity than if I did it myself. I might visit five sites; my agent might visit 5,000." This explosion in agent-driven traffic is fundamentally reshaping internet infrastructure requirements.

Cloudflare has pivoted aggressively into AI infrastructure. Workers AI, AI Gateway, and agent security are now central offerings — the company's distributed edge network, with servers in over 330 cities, is uniquely positioned to serve as the connective tissue for the agentic internet. Prince argues the underlying internet business model (advertising, which requires human eyeballs) will change radically over the next five years as bots increasingly dominate traffic.

On the more controversial side: Cloudflare recently "rightsized" its team to adapt to the AI era. Prince was candid about the difficulty of the decision, framing it as one that almost every company will face in the next 6-12 months. He also reflected on Cloudflare's unconventional early growth — their first major customer segments were human rights organizations and hacktivist communities in regions with poor infrastructure, not Silicon Valley tech companies. That origin story shaped how they built their global network.

Direct quote: "We actually had bot traffic pass human traffic online in the first half of twenty twenty six. In five years you might have a thousand times as much traffic on the internet as you do today."

Claude Blog — Anthropic
"Building intelligent apps for Apple platforms with Claude in the Foundation Models framework"

Anthropic released a new Swift package that lets Apple developers use Apple's Foundation Models framework to call Claude for complex workflows. Apple's framework already enables fast, local on-device tasks (summarization, extraction) in just a few lines of code — now developers can hand off to Claude when a request requires multi-step reasoning, code generation, web search, or code execution. The integration streams Claude's response back into the same view, and because Apple's framework returns typed Swift values via @Generable annotations, developers arrive at the Claude API call with clean, structured inputs. Works on iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, visionOS 27, and watchOS 27.
