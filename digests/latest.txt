The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "Stripe's AI Chief: How AI Agents Will Buy, Sell, and Pay"
Published: 2026-07-09

Takeaway: Emily Sands, head of data and AI at Stripe, maps out the full infrastructure stack needed before AI agents can autonomously buy, sell, and run businesses — and identifies token theft as the most underappreciated risk in the entire AI ecosystem.

A year ago, the conversation about AI agents as buyers was largely hypothetical. Today, Stripe has real infrastructure deployed and real companies building on it. Emily laid out a spectrum of agentic commerce ranging from "level one" — where a human makes all the decisions and an agent simply handles checkout — to fully autonomous, where an agent discovers a service, decides to buy it, and executes the transaction with no human in the loop. By her read, most consumer AI commerce today hovers around level two, with a hint of level three.

The infrastructure Stripe has built to support this spectrum includes three layers: businesses exposing their product catalogs and pricing in machine-readable form, consumers authorizing agents to pay on their behalf, and agents being able to safely execute those transactions. On the partnership side, Stripe is enabling merchants to sell inside Google's AI Mode and Gemini app, inside Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT, and is powering checkout inside Meta's ad surfaces. Early brand adopters include Best Buy, Coach, URBN, and Kate Spade.

The most striking part of the conversation was Emily's discussion of token theft — what she calls "the dine and dash, but for tokens." More than one in six signups at AI companies are fraudulent: not to steal money or credentials, but to steal API token compute. The scale surprised even her when she looked at the data, and she considers it the most underdiscussed topic in all of AI right now.

Looking further out, Emily described the end state she's working toward: an agent that's not just helping you check out faster, but one delegated to run a whole business — buying inputs, selling outputs, managing margin — with a human setting the strategy and the agent handling execution. "It's not Emily permissioning an agent to buy on her behalf. It's Emily has an agent who's tasked with running a business."

Direct quote: "Fraudsters have figured out that in AI, you actually don't really need to steal money or credentials. You can just steal tokens. And the scale of this actually shocked me when I looked at the data. So more than one in six signups at AI companies are this kind of abuse."
